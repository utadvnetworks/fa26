### Communication

We will use [Ed](https://edstem.org/us/courses/100553/) for course announcements, general questions, assignment clarifications, student discussions, and other course-related communication.

***

### Office Hours

The instructor and TA will hold regular office hours to help with course material, paper discussions, research projects, and related concerns. Current times and locations are listed on the [course homepage]({{ '/' | relative_url }}).

To make office hours productive and fair for everyone:

* **Come prepared:** Review the relevant material and make a genuine effort before attending. For project questions, bring a specific question, relevant context, and a description of what you have already tried. The course staff will guide you toward solutions but will not write project code or reports for you.
* **Respect the queue:** Keep each turn focused so everyone has an opportunity to receive help. You are welcome to rejoin the queue if you need additional time.

***

### Course Organization

#### Paper Reading Responses

The reading list is posted on the [schedule]({{ '/schedule/' | relative_url }}) page. Readings marked **[R]** are required, while readings marked **[O]** are optional.

For each class with required readings, submit a response on [Ed](https://edstem.org/us/courses/100553/) by **11:59 PM on the day before class**. Your response should briefly summarize each required paper, identify its key ideas or design choices, and offer your own critical assessment.

#### In-Class Paper Discussions

Students will lead assigned paper discussions. Presentation slides are optional. The goal is to provide the necessary context, explain the paper's central ideas and tradeoffs, and keep the class discussion moving rather than simply summarizing the paper section by section.

#### Research Project

The course project is an open-ended research project completed individually or in groups of two. A list of potential project ideas is available [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Xg-3SyGgReQNZsVCMINAjBHkuqiwNQ7Vx6vSeaeMNEc/edit?usp=sharing).

Each project will include a proposal, progress discussions with the course staff, a final report, and an in-class presentation. Project milestones and deadlines are listed on the [schedule]({{ '/schedule/' | relative_url }}) and will be announced on Ed.

***

### Midterm

The midterm will cover the topics discussed during the first half of the semester. Questions will assess your understanding of the course concepts and the research papers we have studied. For each paper, you should understand:

* The problem the paper aims to solve
* Why that problem is important or challenging
* The paper's key ideas and approach
* How those ideas address the stated problem

***

### Grading

Your final grade will be based on the following components:

* **Paper reading responses (20%)**
* **In-class paper discussion leadership (20%)**
* **Research project, final presentation, and report (50%)**
* **In-class participation (10%)**

Grades will be posted on [Canvas](https://utexas.instructure.com/courses/1451702).

**Important notes:**

* There will be no extra-credit opportunities offered at the end of the semester.
* Grades will not be curved or adjusted based on individual circumstances.

### Deadlines, Late Submissions, and Absences

Plan ahead for reading responses and project milestones. If you anticipate a conflict, contact the instructor **at least one week in advance** so that appropriate arrangements can be considered.

For emergencies such as medical or family situations, contact the instructor as soon as possible. Requests submitted only after a deadline or missed class may not be accepted unless the circumstances prevented earlier notice.

Regular attendance is expected and contributes to your learning and participation grade. If you must miss class, notify the instructor in advance when possible.

### Course Policies

#### Academic Integrity

All submitted course materials, including reading responses, project code, reports, and presentation materials, must represent your own work. When using external sources, cite them properly and clearly indicate which parts are your own. If you are unsure whether something violates this policy, consult the instructor before submitting it.

Violations of academic integrity will be handled according to the [University of Texas policies and procedures](https://deanofstudents.utexas.edu/conduct/index.php).

#### Services for Students with Disabilities

The university is committed to providing an accessible and inclusive learning environment. If you encounter barriers to learning, please inform the instructor so we can work together to ensure that you have an equal opportunity to participate fully in the course.

If you have a disability or need accommodations, contact [Disability and Access (D&A)](https://disability.utexas.edu/). If you are already registered with D&A, provide your Accommodation Letter as early as possible so we can discuss your approved accommodations and needs.

#### Sharing of Course Materials

**Course materials may not be shared outside the class without written permission from the instructor.** This includes lecture materials, course assignments, and student work. Unauthorized sharing may facilitate academic misconduct and violate university policy.

Materials associated with suspected unauthorized sharing may be reported to Student Conduct and Academic Integrity and may result in academic-misconduct charges and sanctions.

#### Syllabus Changes

The instructor reserves the right to modify the syllabus during the semester. Any changes will be announced in class, posted on Ed, and reflected on the course website.
